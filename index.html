About

Hi, I'm Eniola — I edit videos and build content that makes people stop, watch, and feel something.

I'm a freelance video editor and content creator based in Lagos with 2+ years of experience working across social media content, brand videos, event films, and personal milestone highlights — weddings, graduations, birthday reels, you name it.

I don't just cut footage. I think about the hook, the pacing, the visual energy, and the story behind every project. I've worked with creators, brands, organisations, and individuals who all had one thing in common — they wanted their content to actually connect.

Open to one-off projects, creator collabs, retainers, and everything in between. Remote-friendly. Available across Nigeria and internationally.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of projects do you take on? +
Pretty much everything video — short-form social content, creator collabs, wedding and graduation highlights, brand videos, event recap films, and motion graphics. If you're not sure whether your project fits, just reach out.
Can you help me come up with content ideas? +
Yes! I can help you develop content ideas and hooks that fit your niche, audience, and platform — especially useful for creators who want consistent content without burning out on planning.
How long does a project take? +
Short-form content (Reels, TikToks) is usually delivered in 2–3 days. Event films and milestone highlights typically take 4–7 days. Rush delivery is available — just let me know upfront.
How do I send you my footage? +
Google Drive or WeTransfer — whichever works for you. I'll confirm everything before starting and manage the full post-production workflow from there.
Do you work with international clients? +
Absolutely. I work fully remotely with clients across Nigeria and internationally. Everything is handled online from brief to final delivery.
What software do you use? +
CapCut PC (Advanced) is my primary editing tool. I also use DaVinci Resolve for colour grading and Canva for graphic design and social media assets.
